(02) — About

Meet Dr. Tahmina

14+ Years

Dr. Tahmina Sultana Rahman has spent more than fourteen years caring for women through every stage of life. She completed her MBBS in Dhaka, trained in Obstetrics & Gynaecology through the FCPS programme, earned the MRCOG in the United Kingdom, and later pursued a fellowship in minimally invasive gynaecologic surgery. Today she serves as Consultant and Associate Professor, combining hands-on clinical practice with the teaching of future specialists.

Her philosophy is simple: the diagnosis begins with listening. Appointments are never rushed, and no question is considered too small or too personal. Dr. Tahmina takes the time to understand each woman’s history, worries and priorities before recommending anything, because the best plan is the one a patient understands and feels part of.

Patients can expect a calm, respectful consultation, clear explanations in Bangla or English, and recommendations grounded in current evidence. Where surgery is needed, she favours gentle keyhole techniques whenever they are suitable. Throughout, your privacy is protected and your decisions are respected, from the first visit to long-term follow-up.

Principles of care

    Options, benefits and limits are shared openly, so the final choice is always yours.

(03) — Services

Complete care, every stage

From the first antenatal visit to life after menopause, Dr. Tahmina offers specialist care for every stage of a woman’s health, delivered with clear explanations and respect for your choices.

Showing 8 of 8 services

  • 01 Pregnancy

    Antenatal & Pregnancy Care

    Regular, reassuring check-ups from the first weeks of pregnancy to the day you deliver. Dr. Tahmina monitors you and your baby closely and answers every question along the way.

    What it includes

    • Pregnancy confirmation and dating scan
    • Routine screening and growth monitoring
    • Nutrition and lifestyle guidance
    • Birth planning conversations

    Typical visit30–45 min

  • 02 Pregnancy

    High-Risk Pregnancy Management

    Extra attention for pregnancies that need closer monitoring, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, twins or a previous complication. The aim is a safe pregnancy with a clear plan at every step.

    What it includes

    • Detailed risk assessment
    • More frequent visits and scans
    • Coordination with other specialists
    • Timing and place-of-birth planning

    Typical visit45–60 min

  • 03 Pregnancy

    Normal & Cesarean Delivery

    Supportive, safety-first delivery care, whether you plan a vaginal birth or need a cesarean. Your preferences are discussed in advance and respected wherever it is safe to do so.

    What it includes

    • Labour monitoring and support
    • Planned and emergency cesarean care
    • Immediate postnatal checks
    • Recovery and feeding guidance

    Typical visit30–45 min

  • 04 Surgery

    Laparoscopic & Hysteroscopic Surgery

    Keyhole techniques that use very small incisions, or none at all, to diagnose and treat many gynaecological conditions. Suitable patients often enjoy a quicker recovery and a shorter hospital stay.

    What it includes

    • Assessment of suitability for keyhole surgery
    • Fibroids, cysts and endometriosis care
    • Diagnostic hysteroscopy of the uterus
    • Pre- and post-operative guidance

    Typical visit30–45 min

  • 05 Hormonal & Wellness

    Infertility Evaluation & Treatment

    A compassionate, step-by-step approach for couples finding it hard to conceive. Causes are explored carefully and treatment options are explained honestly, so you always know what to expect.

    What it includes

    • Detailed fertility history and examination
    • Ovulation tracking and tests
    • Scans and tubal assessment
    • Personalised treatment planning

    Typical visit45–60 min

  • 06 Hormonal & Wellness

    PCOS & Menstrual Disorders

    Practical help for irregular, painful or heavy periods and for polycystic ovary syndrome. Treatment focuses on finding the cause and combining medical care with realistic lifestyle support.

    What it includes

    • Cycle and symptom review
    • Hormone tests and pelvic ultrasound
    • Diet and activity guidance
    • Long-term management plan

    Typical visit30–40 min

  • 07 Hormonal & Wellness

    Menopause & Hormonal Health

    Understanding support for the changes that come with perimenopause and menopause, from hot flushes and sleep problems to bone and heart health. Options are tailored to your symptoms and history.

    What it includes

    • Symptom and risk assessment
    • Discussion of treatment options
    • Bone health and heart health advice
    • Lifestyle and wellbeing support

    Typical visit30–40 min

  • 08 Preventive

    Adolescent & Preventive Women’s Health

    A friendly, judgement-free space for teenagers and women of every age to ask questions and stay ahead of problems. Prevention, screening and honest education keep small concerns from growing.

    What it includes

    • Cervical screening and HPV vaccination advice
    • Puberty and period guidance
    • Contraception counselling
    • Annual well-woman check-up

    Typical visit20–30 min

Every treatment plan is tailored to the individual after a full clinical assessment. Outcomes differ from patient to patient, and recommendations are always discussed with you first.

The best time is as soon as you know you are pregnant, ideally between 6 and 10 weeks. An early visit lets us confirm the pregnancy, estimate your due date, review your health history, talk about nutrition and supplements, and plan your scans and tests. If you have an existing medical condition, had complications in a previous pregnancy, or are planning to conceive, a pre-pregnancy visit is even better. This is general guidance; your own schedule is tailored after a consultation.

Please bring a photo ID, any previous reports, scans and prescriptions, the names of medicines and supplements you currently take, and the date your last period started. If you are pregnant, bring your antenatal card and earlier ultrasound reports. A short list of your symptoms or questions helps us make the most of your time. For a first visit, arriving about 10 minutes early keeps things relaxed.

Online Video Consultations run Saturday to Thursday, 10:00 AM–12:00 PM, and cost ৳1,200. After you book, our team sends a secure video link by WhatsApp or email with a short checklist. Online visits suit follow-ups, report reviews and many first discussions, though Dr. Tahmina may advise an in-person examination when it is needed. They are not meant for emergencies.

A Pap test is a quick screening of the cervix. Try to book it for a time when you are not menstruating and, unless you are told otherwise, avoid intercourse, vaginal creams and douching for about two days beforehand. Wear comfortable clothing, tell us if you are or might be pregnant, and share any symptoms or worries. Most women find it brief, and if you feel anxious you can say so and we will go at your pace. Instructions can differ from person to person, so please follow the guidance you receive at your appointment.

Recovery depends on the procedure and on each patient, so Dr. Tahmina gives personal instructions before you go home. In general, laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery uses smaller incisions, and many women return to light daily activities sooner than after open surgery. Expect some tiredness and mild discomfort for a few days, a gradual return to normal routines, and a follow-up visit to review your progress. Contact us promptly if you notice fever, worsening pain, heavy bleeding or anything that worries you. This is general information, not a promise of any particular recovery timeline.
